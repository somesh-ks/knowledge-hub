## 9. Scientific & Academic Publications (HCI & Interaction Design - Open Access)

Automatable journal RSS feeds and daily preprint streams. Annual conferences, bridge publications, and research lab pages are in [Sources Reference.md §5](../docs/reference/sources-reference.md).

### Open Access Archival Journals (Peer-Reviewed)
| Publication | Publisher / URL | Focus & Signal | Access |
|-------------|-----------------|----------------|--------|
| **ACM TOCHI** (Transactions on Computer-Human Interaction) | https://dl.acm.org/journal/tochi | The premier archival journal in HCI globally. Foundational papers on interaction paradigms, sensory feedback, cognitive engineering, and intelligent UI. | Fully Open Access (ACM Open 2026) |
| **ACM PACMHCI** (Proceedings of the ACM on HCI) | https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmhci | Gold-standard journal-first publication series for CSCW, EICS, GROUP, ISS, and MobileHCI papers. | Fully Open Access |

### Preprints & Open Academic Feeds
| Stream | URL / Feed | Signal |
|--------|------------|--------|
| **arXiv cs.HC (Human-Computer Interaction)** | https://arxiv.org/list/cs.HC/recent | Real-time preprints of accepted papers before conference embargoes. Best early radar for generative AI UI and novel interaction patterns. |
| **ACM DL RSS Feeds** | https://dl.acm.org/action/showFeed?type=etoc&feed=rss&jc=tochi | Automated XML feed of newly published TOCHI and UIST papers. |
