# Settings and forms

Ledger section 3, one surface. Read [the core](../design-engineering-ledger.md) first (taste in section 1, how to work in section 2, the phone in section 11). Each row: the ask, what was built, the decision; if a decision and a row disagree, the decision wins.

## Settings and forms
- **A label is 12px above its field, the fields 24px apart (owner, 2026-10-29, D229).** `--field-label-gap` in `.manage-field` and `.sheet-field`; the resting outline is `--field-border`, grey, and only focus is blue, so an autofocused field (the admin sign-in) opens blue by design.
- **Edit sheets save on Save only (2026-10-25).** The block, category and source, and Block sources sheets have one primary button; the scrim, Close, Escape, a pull and Back ask "Save changes?" (Save, Discard, Keep editing) when a field changed, by `sheetGuard.ts` over the confirm dialog (`.confirm-actions.is-stacked`).
- **Structure.**
  - Two levels, one job per page, a breadcrumb title, autosave (D51).
  - Rows hover as rounded tiles clear of the hairlines.
  - A row's value sits at its end, not under its name, which read as squeezed; summaries are short ("Every other day at 7", a source count) and rows with nothing to say have no line; podcast topic chips go by name; an unticked chip in a card has its own tone, as dark mode's raised tone equalled the card (2026-10-22).
  - Category page, 2026-10-25: the source-type chips are tabs again (All, then one per type present; `.tabs-in-card` in the search block, `SourceFilter.astro`). The block sticks at every width under `--sticky-top`, desktop included. No segmented selector, no Blocked tab (blocked sources live on their own page).
  - A card's row tiles are concentric with it (its buttons were too until D186 made them pills), radius = card radius less the gap: 24, 16 (8px in), 8 (16px in). A one-row card (about 72px) steps down to 24px, its tiles 16px: 32px read as a pill, then 16px as too sharp (owner, 2026-10-27, twice; `.card-group:not(:has(> * + *))`; designSystem.spec holds every card's corner under 40% of its height). Chips, select chips, select values and segmented controls keep their pill inside a card (D167; on 2026-10-22 they too were concentric, measured within 1px with `component.card`).
  - A Sources row and a source row sit 16px in, the search field's own padding, so the emoji and the chevron line up with the field's glyph and clear button (owner, 2026-10-23); the chevron was pulled out to the edge before (`topics.css .row-action`, `.source-row`). Their hover tile is the row's own box, on the heading's and the field's edges, where it reached 16px past both, to a phone's edge ("optical alignment of list's hover state and search bar and heading", owner, 2026-10-24), and fully round, as the rail's rows and the field are ("full curved borders so it is consistent with the rest of the design language", owner, 2026-10-24; `component.row.tile-radius`). In a card a tile keeps the card's inner radius.
  - One left text edge, roomier cards: "the padding around the list menus in settings page and other pages must be spacious and the text elements must be horizontally aligned to the left to have optical cleannes" (owner, 2026-10-22). The title, group heads and notes outside a card start the card's padding in, so they share the rows' edge (`settings.css`, any `.settings-view` or `.manage` holding a `.card-group` or Change log entry). The card's words went from 16 to 24px in, its tiles and select fills from 8 to 16px in on every side (the card adds 8px above and below, each tile takes 8px inside its 64px row), so the radii became 32, 16 (16px in), 8 (24px in). Rows keep 64px. Measured at 390 and 1280px on Settings, Daily, Weekly, Preferences, About, Privacy, Sounds, Voice, Change log, Costs and Blocked sources: one edge, 0px spread (16px before). Sources and a topic page have no `.card-group` and lead with an icon, which holds the edge (`primitives.css .card-group`, `component.card`). On Blocked sources the field's label, its words and its error take the same edge, its outline hanging out as a card's does (`blocked.css`). A topic page's Listen in line is pulled out by the card's padding, its select and the source tiles sit one inset in at the tile radius (`topics.css`). The owner kept it (2026-10-23): a Settings title sits 24px further in than Library's and Latest's, so it moves between tabs; one edge within a page wins. Reversed (owner, 2026-10-26: "Settings has more margin than Latest and Library, on web and mobile"): one gutter across pages wins. The title, heads and notes start on the page gutter and a card's outer edge is that edge; only the card's rows' words sit its padding in. A view root (`.settings-view`, `.library`, `.manage`) takes its inline edges from the shared layout, never its own: styles.test.ts fails a view-root rule with inline padding, inline margin or a max width, and designSystem.spec.ts compares the title's and the content's edges across Latest, Library, Settings, Preferences and a category.
  - On/off for Daily and Weekly (owner, 2026-10-26, Q16): a switch row ("Daily briefing", "Weekly briefing") tops each page. Off, the other controls are disabled and dimmed, not hidden, and a quiet line says "Off: no daily briefing is made". It saves live through the schedule op (`enabled`, top level for Daily, `weekly.enabled` for Weekly); the engine, cron route and health skip a briefing that is off (`PodcastProfile.astro`, `podcast.css .podcast-body`).
  - The score is a star and a number (D103). A Poor or zero rating takes neutral ink, never the danger colour (2026-10-25: a low score measures what a source sent; volume is a confidence weight in `sourceScore.ts`, not a multiplier).
  - The edit source panel uses token spacing, with "Scorecard" on the left and "x.x/5" on the right, and an empty state (#122).
  - 2026-10-24: the scorecard pads 24px and "x.x/5" matches the title's size; the panel gains a "Favorite source" checkbox (high_signal, `.edit-favorite`).
- **Saving.**
  - No toast while saving or once saved; the control shows the change and a screen reader hears "Saved." (D115).
  - Saved values show for three minutes before the build (D79).
- **Selects.**
  - Every select is `appearance: base-select` with a drawn list (D51).
  - Long lists get a searchable picker: about 55 zones as "Stockholm (GMT+2)", voices by name, gender and accent (D101). The offset is computed at an instant, daylight saving included (`packages/core/src/time.ts:78`), because "time zone should be mentioned... GMT plus or minus" (2026-10-12).
  - Voices are auditioned in place (D105); the owner also asked for preview in the New episode form (2026-10-12).
  - A voice's accent flag is an SVG from one sprite (`public/flags.svg`, Flagpack, MIT, 37 regions, 90 KB), drawn by the picker in the list and the trigger at 16 by 12px with the 4px radius (`picker.css .flag`); Windows has no flag emoji and showed two letters ("country flags are not rendered well on windows", owner, 2026-10-25). A region without a flag (Saudi Arabia's arms alone weigh 136 KB; Indonesia, Israel, Kenya, Taiwan were cut for the budget) shows a small letter badge, never a broken image.
- **Lists.**
  - Lists come first with their counts; add forms sit in `<details>`; repeated hints and the Generate row went (2026-10-08).
  - "N topics" heads the list with a mid-size Add on the same row; a topic shows seven sources, then "Show N more" (2026-10-13). Reversed by D125: no count, no filters, a floating Add on a phone and an Add at the end of the search row from 64rem.
  - Headings: never bold, anywhere; Settings group heads are 18 to 20px regular H2s (22px was too big), apart from the title by size and space only (D125). A topic's sliders fold into Topic weightage, Formats and Perspective groups; its formats are tabs, All first; Listen in sits in the card (D125).
  - Daily and Weekly share one component, so a change lands on both (2026-10-13).
  - Topics are always available: no locked or disabled choices. A locked chip at opacity 0.32 read at about 1.5:1 (2026-10-12).
  - Source icons come through `/api/site-icon` with a letter fallback, so the policy keeps `img-src 'self' data:` (`pages/api/site-icon.ts`); widening `img-src` to `https:` was rejected (2026-10-09).
  - The add-topic form uses `novalidate` with inline messages; an emoji-only name no longer becomes the id "topic" (2026-10-08).
  - Model headings sit on the left in regular weight (D113, #125). The emoji picker's section labels are h3s and were bold by the browser's default; they are regular (audit, 2026-10-22, `topics.css .emoji-label`).
  - The emoji picker's list was late (owner, 2026-10-24): drawing all 1,878 emoji in one task held a cold open for 1.3 s on a desktop. Each cell now renders only near the view (`content-visibility`), the list fills in batches of 120 after the first category, and hovering, pressing or focusing the button preloads it (`emojiPicker.ts`, `topics.css .emoji-cell`).
  - Each emoji sits in its cell's optical centre: a pad of a tenth of the font size moves the emoji font's ink off the UI font's baseline, the same for every glyph (owner, 2026-10-24; exempt in `designSystem.spec.ts`).
  - An emoji cell takes the standard hover state layer under a fine pointer (`.hover-fill`, D72); the chosen emoji's fill and the focus ring win over it (owner, 2026-10-24).
  - The Listen in list on a phone opened under the sticky search block (one under the header), hiding Catch up; the card that holds the open list is lifted to the header's level (audit, 2026-10-22, `topics.css`).
  - Unticked chips in a `.card-group` take the overlay tone, not the raised one: in dark the raised tone equals the card's, so Daily and Weekly's topic chips read as bare text (audit, 2026-10-22, `primitives.css`).
  - The Podcast voice subtitle is only "Daily, Weekly and Catch up episodes are read in these voices." (owner, 2026-10-21); the "Weekly keeps the voice it had before" note is gone (2026-10-22).
  - Blocked sources counts and asks in sources: "1 source", "Block a source" (audit, 2026-10-22). The route stays `/settings/blocked-outlets`. Owner pass of 2026-10-25: the label, the note and the "example.com" placeholder went; the field ("Search sources") filters the list, and Block opens a sheet that searches the catalogue (the create sheet: full screen on a phone, a side panel from 40rem) and takes a pasted address; the field and Block sit one card inset in, on the rows' edge ("the optical balance on left and right is broken"); nothing is hidden until the script runs, so a refresh no longer flashes the field and buttons (`blocked.css`, `blockedSheet.ts`).
  - Sliders (owner, 2026-10-25): every settings range is capped at `--slider-max-width` (10rem) so the names keep focus; a weight row was a grid with the stop's name over the track's start, reverted on 2026-10-27 at the owner's request to the name and stop on one line above the slider; a card's rows end 16px from its edge so a select's edge meets the card's rhythm; a topic page's divider between suggested and active sources runs full bleed; the topic page says "N sources active" and the Sources list hint is the total alone ("18 of 20 sources on" could only mean the total). Weights moved by votes say "Adjusted" (D190). Daily and Weekly say "Max length".
  - About has "About the author" (Somesh, a LinkedIn link) and two lines on what Singularity is, no stack names (owner, 2026-10-25). Privacy names each service with a link to its policy and a "Last updated" date; Change log has no subtitle (owner, 2026-10-25).
  - Sources and Preferences are a segmented switch on a topic page (`?view=preferences`); one global type mix on `/settings/preferences`; a small "Settings /" line above each subpage title (`.settings-crumb`, owner 2026-10-25, reverses the "no breadcrumbs" of D129; the link is the shared `.text-link` with a 44px target); Theme is a select; Weekly has no switch (D129, 2026-10-21).
  - Settings rows (owner, 2026-10-25): a label is as wide as its words and the control takes the row end, so hovering the empty middle lights nothing; in a card a select's tile ends 12px from the card edge, as far as it starts from the top and bottom (`--value-select-pull`, settings.css).
  - Review caught a topics tab strip that zoomed Chrome out (a grid column without `minmax(0, 1fr)`) and cramped panel rows, which led to the 8pt spacing system.
