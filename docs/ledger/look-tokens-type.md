# Look, tokens and type

Ledger section 3, one surface. Read [the core](../design-engineering-ledger.md) first (taste in section 1, how to work in section 2, the phone in section 11). Each row: the ask, what was built, the decision; if a decision and a row disagree, the decision wins.

## Look, tokens and type
- **Look.** Hand-written CSS from DTCG tokens, Base UI for accessible parts; Tailwind and shadcn rejected as a templated look (D4). The first ask was "a light ui library to keep things elegant and high quality" (2026-10-05).
  - Pipeline: `tokens/tokens.json` to `scripts/buildTokens.ts` to the generated `styles/tokens.css`.
  - Tests check token drift, tiers and contrast. Tokens for type, colour and spacing were an explicit ask; 18 raw values were tokenised in one pass (2026-10-08).
  - The first identity (2026-10-05) was a reading page built as the audio timeline: a vertical rail with a node per story that filled while playing, Schibsted Grotesk with Literata, a Lingonberry accent, no cards and no hairlines. It gave way to the Google Podcasts redesign (D31), then to Material tonal surfaces and Google Sans Flex (D72). The rail survives only in the Generate step list.
- **Tonal language** (D72, owner: "a real Google app"):
  - `surface-container` for the page's own controls.
  - `accent-container` for chosen or current.
  - `accent-container-high` for actions that are not places, such as the phone's floating New episode (D86); in dark it is blue-700, a lifted step like light's, never a fill (2026-10-23; blue-910 before D238). In the rail New episode took `surface-container` with accent ink, so beside the current row it was not a second selection (owner, 2026-10-22).
  - `accent-action` for the rail's Catch up (`.btn-action-tonal`, buttons.css; owner, 2026-10-27: "looks very faded" in both themes). A saturated blue in both themes (owner, same day, of the greyed blue-475 and blue-650 it replaced: "looks terrible in light mode", "desaturated" in dark): light blue-500 with white words, a step brighter than the full accent (blue-600); dark blue-400 with blue-900 words (the owner's pick). It must not swap with the current row's fill, which alone means "you are here". Fill to page 4.38 light, 5.11 dark (at least 3:1, so it reads as a shape); words 4.64 and 5.12, at least 4.5 in every state. A 16px rounded rectangle (`--action-tonal-radius`), the one recorded exception to the pill rule; no elevation, no shadow. Hover and press move the fill away from its words, toward blue-900 in light and blue-50 in dark (pure white turned the hovered fill violet), so the words gain contrast in every state. On /create it takes the current row's fill.
  - State layers: hover 8%, press 12% of the control's ink. A chip on a card hovers at half that in light (`--card-chip-hover`): the full layer took a white chip to L 94 on the card's L 95 and it melted in (owner, 2026-10-24).
  - Pills for buttons (but the rail's Catch up, a 16px rectangle, above), chips, select chips, select values, segmented controls and the search field, in a card as on the page, and for every select in a form (D169: the Create sheet, the Add panel, the multi-select); text fields, a card's buttons and the floating button too, a multi-line field 24px (D186, 2026-10-24) (D167, 2026-10-23: "that shouldn't be possible in a design system"). For one day (2026-10-22) chips and select values in a card took the card's concentric radius; the owner reversed it on the phone. 12px rows, outlines only on text fields (3:1, D54).
- **Controls are components with their real names** (owner, 2026-10-29, D237: "whatever the components you see and the design system library, are the components that are being used ... components named not by how they are used in the app, rather by their actual names ... all the buttons must be collected in one section ... very many duplicates which can be consolidated"). Button is first: `design-system/Button.astro` draws Filled, Tonal and Text (`button-filled`, `button-tonal`, `button-text`; were `btn-primary`, `btn-secondary`, `btn`) and a script or island takes the class from `design-system/button.ts`; one thing on screen changed (every other element's computed style at rest, hover, press and focus, on 25 pages at 390 and 1280, light and dark, matches): the Delete confirm buttons on the categories and sources pages were blue because `episode-actions.css` loaded before `BaseLayout.css` and `.btn` beat `.confirm-delete`; as the `danger` tone they are red, as written. The gates: a hand-written class, a class with no rule or a rule no component draws fails `designSystem.test.ts`; a PR that touches the system needs the `design-system` label and holds nothing else (`scripts/designSystemGate.mjs`); CODEOWNERS names the owner. Left: Icon button (`icon-btn*`, `dock-btn`, `transport-btn`, `view-btn`, `source-plus-btn`), FAB (`create-fab`, `play-fab`, `add-fab`, and `btn-action-tonal`, the Catch up colour), Chip (`chip`, `filter-chip`), Link (`text-link`, `source-link`, `player-source-link`, `run-link`, `run-details`, `edit-address-link`, `story-link`, `skip-link`); `aside-link`, `card-link` and `speed-btn` are named when their family is.
- **Type.**
  - One family: Google Sans Flex variable, latin only, with a metric-matched Arial fallback.
  - Two weights, regular and medium; a test bans a third (D90).
  - Story and summary 16px at every width; headline 18 to 20px; meta 14px; section headings 22px regular; display at most 32px; reading leading 1.55 (D73).
  - A quiet line goes under its title, never above it (D31); a story's publisher is the one exception, above its headline (D161).
  - Summaries and highlight text take `on-surface-reading`, one ink step under the headline (light grey-700, dark grey-200): size and weight are fixed, so contrast lets the headline lead; AAA on the page, tested (2026-10-23).
  - `text-wrap: balance` on headings, kept working by `inline-block` rather than `nowrap` (D93).
- **Spacing and shape.**
  - 4px base, nine steps from 0.25 to 6rem, each at least 25% larger than the last (test).
  - Radii xs to sheet, with concentric radii tested (outer = inner + padding).
  - Reading measure 34rem (D86).
  - Every page starts 64px down from 40rem (D102).
  - Pages share one top rhythm to 1px (`e2e/rhythm.spec.ts`).
- **The style guide** (2026-10-29, D226). The owner: the design system "should move out on its own", with search, categorised components, a component page with state controls and thumbnails that react to hover (m3.material.io), in the app's own design language. Then: the guide must "reflect the actual component library" with "100% parity", components named by what they are (a "player button" is a FAB; all buttons in one section, all links in another), duplicates merged, design-system changes in their own pull requests that he merges. Then, on the first look: better icons, a dark mode toggle, "Singularity" with a small "Design" chip, no link back to the reader, less on screen, an accordion side menu only where it earns its place (Components: atoms, molecules, organisms), the filters as the app's chips with the rest in a drop-down, per-component coverage and light analytics with the method documented, a theming engine where a few variables change the whole colour system, expressive parts as in Material 3 Expressive, the build day bottom left, and a theme toggle (System, Light, Dark) there too. Second look (13:54): the theme switch as one icon button, dark by default; the guide helps pick components by decision points; a visual Overview like Material's, with no search there but a filter on Colour and each tab; navigation as Overview, Principles, Foundations (Colour, Typography, Motion, Tokens), Components; "Design" as plain text, not cropped; WCAG and OKLCH values on colour; icons that fit (a palette for colour, Aa for type). Built: all of that, the date and the theme button at the foot. Not built: the accordion for Components (no components yet), filter chips with a drop-down and sort, coverage analytics, the theming engine, expressive parts, the components themselves (they wait for real components, D226).
- **The Taste page** (2026-10-29 to 10-30). The owner asked for his taste as reusable values beside Material 3's, no transcripts, short and simple, with a visual hierarchy and a subtitle that can be read; copy that does not sound AI-written; the blue icon circles liked, then blue on the icon only over a neutral disc and smaller on desktop; values grouped so they can be glanced at, a search on both widths, accordions on a phone with only the first open and none on desktop; cards, not rows of text; Material 3's differences and its expressive ideas as two titled groups; a nicer count by each heading; every star an outline. Built, in `apps/design`. Not built: the outline star in the reader's source rating (it stays filled, D103).
