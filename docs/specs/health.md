# Health

The app's health on one page, read live: whether briefings published, how long runs take, how fast pages are for real visitors, what errored, which workflows failed and which services answer. Reasons: D203. The daily check it mirrors: [hardening](../engineering/hardening.md).

## Requirements

- `/admin/health` in the command center (D203, D224; `/settings/health` redirects there), marks first, words where a mark cannot say it (2026-11-04, the owner): a summary card (a state title, Healthy, Degraded or Failing, with its glyph, one line "N flagged · checked 10:00", then one line per flagged reading, worst first); Podcasts (Daily and Weekly as their last 10 runs' columns, each as tall as its run took, the newest right, the median a dashed line, then Catch up, Narration, Model fallbacks); Jobs (each workflow a strip of its week's finished runs, up to 40, oldest first, a failure or cancel in its tone, then how it stands); Speed (a Phones/Desktop segmented control, then LCP, INP and CLS p75 as a pin on Google's three bands); Errors today (a tile per kind, the week under it); Budgets (D360: Cost, Speed, Tests, Bugs, Security, Keys, a tile with its glyph and word, its numbers on hover); Services; then Stage times. Only a reading that needs a look carries a glyph (warning or failing), a budget tile its good one too, and the words say the same, so colour is never the only signal. The page paints this device's last answer at once and repaints from the fresh one (`apps/admin/src/lib/kept.ts`); Sign out forgets it.
- Stage times: a card per kind of run (Daily, Weekly, Catch up), a bar per stage in pipeline order: the median solid, the slowest tenth the lighter reach, scaled to the longest in its card; the median reads at the end. Data is `readStageTimings` (14 days of finished runs, no dry runs) through `GET /admin/api/stages` (`apps/admin/api/stages.ts`, behind the admin gate; needs `DATABASE_URL` and `SINGULARITY_CONFIG_STORE=postgres` on the admin project). 503 with no database reads "Not connected", any other failure "Unavailable", no runs "No finished runs yet".
- `GET /api/health` reads the content store (`state/last-run.json`, `state/source-health.json`, the newest 14 briefings), Neon (field vitals, client faults, the report `health --save` keeps in `health_reports`, in the same wake) and GitHub's run list, each part within 4 s, and judges with `singularity health`'s lines (`@singularity/engine/health-limits`), and carries the series the marks draw (`series`: run times, job runs, vitals with their lines, errors by day and week). A briefing's time is `generated_at` less `window.to` (its run's start), never the record's `at`, which waits for the publish slot. Answers only the command center session cookie, 401 without it (D257); 30 a minute across instances; one answer kept 20 s per instance.

## Scenarios

- WHEN a run started ahead publishes an hour later THEN its run time is its generation time, not the wait.
- WHEN a metric has fewer than 20 visits on a device THEN its reading shows with no dot and "Too few to judge".
- WHEN run.yml's hourly ticks skip THEN they are not counted as runs.
- WHEN Neon, GitHub or the content store does not answer THEN only its rows say "Unavailable" (or "Build copy") and the note names it.
