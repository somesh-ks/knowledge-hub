# Health

The app's health on one page, read live: whether briefings published, how long runs take, how fast pages are for real visitors, what errored, which workflows failed and which services answer. Reasons: D203. The daily check it mirrors: [hardening](../engineering/hardening.md).

## Requirements

- `/admin/health` in the command center (D203, D224; `/settings/health` redirects there). 2026-11-04, the owner: "very hard to get a complete picture", "something health-related", "a bit too long". First the Summary: a heartbeat trace (`apps/admin/src/scripts/healthRing.ts` `drawPulse`, pillars in `apps/admin/src/lib/pillars.ts`), a beat per section below in the tone and shape of its worst check (a clean spike fine, a low uneven beat to check, a drop failing), its name and one word under it; a press shows that section's checks under it, each a link to its section; then "Checked 10:00" and any partial note. Then one section per pillar: Content (Daily, Weekly, Catch up, Narration); Pipeline (one runs card with a Daily/Weekly switch, opening on a kind whose run time needs a look: its last 10 runs as columns, the median a dashed line, then its stage bars; then Jobs, each workflow one line from 40rem with a strip of its week's finished runs, up to 28, a failure or cancel in its tone, then Model fallbacks and Sources); Site (a centred Phones/Desktop switch, LCP, INP and CLS p75 as a pin on Google's three bands, then Page errors and Blocked scripts as "0 today, 2 this week"); Server (Database, Content store, Server errors, Timeouts); Budgets (D360: a tile each, its glyph and word, its numbers on hover). Summary and the sections are section tabs under 80rem and the Contents list beside the column from 80rem, the one in view marked (`apps/admin/src/scripts/sectionSpy.ts`). A line is its name, then its reading with the glyph right before it, on Health and on the Overview, so names keep one edge; only a reading that needs a look carries a glyph, a budget tile its good one too. The page paints this device's last answer at once and repaints from the fresh one (`apps/admin/src/lib/kept.ts`); Sign out forgets it.
- Stage times: under each kind's run columns (a Catch up card is added after the runs card), a bar per stage in pipeline order: the median solid, the slowest tenth the lighter reach, scaled to the longest in its kind. Data is `readStageTimings` (14 days of finished runs, no dry runs) through `GET /admin/api/stages` (`apps/admin/api/stages.ts`, behind the admin gate; needs `DATABASE_URL` and `SINGULARITY_CONFIG_STORE=postgres` on the admin project).
- Every admin page says a failed ask under its title as a DS banner (`apps/admin/src/lib/problem.ts`): what did not load, the status in plain words, and Copy details, which copies the page, the request, the HTTP status and body, Vercel's request id, the time and the browser for an AI agent. Danger when nothing is on screen; warning when this device's last answer still is.
- `GET /api/health` reads the content store (`state/last-run.json`, `state/source-health.json`, the newest 14 briefings), Neon (field vitals, client faults, the report `health --save` keeps in `health_reports`, in the same wake) and GitHub's run list, each part within 4 s, and judges with `singularity health`'s lines (`@singularity/engine/health-limits`), and carries the series the marks draw (`series`: run times, job runs, vitals with their lines, errors by day and week). A briefing's time is `generated_at` less `window.to` (its run's start), never the record's `at`, which waits for the publish slot. Answers only the command center session cookie, 401 without it (D257); 30 a minute across instances; one answer kept 20 s per instance.

## Scenarios

- WHEN a run started ahead publishes an hour later THEN its run time is its generation time, not the wait.
- WHEN a metric has fewer than 20 visits on a device THEN its reading shows with no dot and "Too few to judge".
- WHEN run.yml's hourly ticks skip THEN they are not counted as runs.
- WHEN Neon, GitHub or the content store does not answer THEN only its rows say "Unavailable" (or "Build copy") and the note names it.
- WHEN an ask fails THEN its banner shows until the next answer, and its details name the request id.
