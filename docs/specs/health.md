# Health

The app's health on one page, read live: whether briefings published, how long runs take, how fast pages are for real visitors, what errored, which workflows failed and which services answer. Reasons: D203. The daily check it mirrors: [hardening](../engineering/hardening.md).

## Requirements

- `/admin/health` in the command center (D203, D224; `/settings/health` redirects there). 2026-11-04, the owner: "very hard to get a complete picture", "something health-related", "a bit too long". First the Summary (2026-11-05, the owner picked tiles over the heartbeat; then "no blue boxes", and a person must understand what is wrong and hand it to an AI agent): DS stat tiles (`apps/admin/src/scripts/healthSummary.ts`, pillars in `apps/admin/src/lib/pillars.ts`), Overall then one per section, two a row on a phone and three from 40rem, each its name with its tone's glyph at the far edge, its state in words ("Fine", "2 to check", "1 failing", "4 to look at" when both), then how many of its checks are fine, tinted in its tone's subtle fill when it needs a look; a section's tile is a link to its section, nothing is picked. Under them, "N things need you" lists every flagged check, worst first in section order: its line (a link to its section), what it means for readers and where to start (`apps/admin/src/lib/healthHelp.ts`, the likely cause, not a diagnosis), its reading's detail, and for an error row the week's three commonest reports (`series.errors[id].top`, quoted as untrusted text); then "Prompts to fix these are in Actions. The other N checks are fine."; then "Checked 10:00" and any partial note. Then Actions (`apps/admin/src/scripts/healthActions.ts`; the owner: "an Action section in which we have all the prompts to copy", "a little monospace font component"): a prompt for an AI agent per flagged check and per part of the page that failed to load, with "Fix all N" first when there are two or more, each its name and a Copy over its whole text in a monospace snippet that scrolls when long. A prompt opens with the check and its reading, then what it means, where to start, the quoted reports, where the check is computed (`apps/web/src/server/health.ts` by id) or which route failed, the repo, when it was read, a warning when the readings are this device's kept ones or the answer was partial, the repo's rules, and what to reply. It follows the banners: a load that fails or recovers later redraws it. "Nothing to fix" when nothing is flagged. Then one section per pillar: Content (Daily, Weekly, Catch up, Narration); Pipeline (one runs card with a Daily/Weekly switch, opening on a kind whose run time needs a look: its last 10 runs as columns, the median a dashed line, then its stage bars; then Jobs, each workflow one line from 40rem with a strip of its week's finished runs, up to 28, a failure or cancel in its tone, then Model fallbacks and Sources); Site (a centred Phones/Desktop switch, LCP, INP and CLS p75 as a pin on Google's three bands, then Page errors and Blocked scripts as "0 today, 2 this week"); Server (Database, Content store, Server errors, Timeouts); Budgets (D360: a tile each like the summary's, its name and glyph, its word, its numbers, tinted when it needs a look). Summary, Actions and the sections are section tabs under 80rem and the Contents list beside the column from 80rem, the one in view marked (`apps/admin/src/scripts/sectionSpy.ts`). A line is its name, then its reading with the glyph right before it, on Health and on the Overview, so names keep one edge; only a reading that needs a look carries a glyph, a tile its good one too. The page paints this device's last answer at once and repaints from the fresh one (`apps/admin/src/lib/kept.ts`); Sign out forgets it.
- Stage times: under each kind's run columns (a Catch up card is added after the runs card), a bar per stage in pipeline order: the median solid, the slowest tenth the lighter reach, scaled to the longest in its kind. Data is `readStageTimings` (14 days of finished runs, no dry runs) through `GET /admin/api/stages` (`apps/admin/api/stages.ts`, behind the admin gate; needs `DATABASE_URL` and `SINGULARITY_CONFIG_STORE=postgres` on the admin project).
- Every admin page says a failed ask under its title as a DS banner (`apps/admin/src/lib/problem.ts`): what did not load, the status in plain words, and Copy details, which copies the page, the request, the HTTP status and body, Vercel's request id, the time and the browser for an AI agent. Danger when nothing is on screen; warning when this device's last answer still is.
- `GET /api/health` reads the content store (`state/last-run.json`, `state/source-health.json`, the newest 14 briefings), Neon (field vitals, client faults, the report `health --save` keeps in `health_reports`, in the same wake) and GitHub's run list, each part within 4 s, and judges with `singularity health`'s lines (`@singularity/engine/health-limits`), and carries the series the marks draw (`series`: run times, job runs, vitals with their lines, errors by day and week). A briefing's time is `generated_at` less `window.to` (its run's start), never the record's `at`, which waits for the publish slot. Answers only the command center session cookie, 401 without it (D257); 30 a minute across instances; one answer kept 20 s per instance.

## Scenarios

- WHEN a run started ahead publishes an hour later THEN its run time is its generation time, not the wait.
- WHEN a metric has fewer than 20 visits on a device THEN its reading shows with no dot and "Too few to judge".
- WHEN run.yml's hourly ticks skip THEN they are not counted as runs.
- WHEN Neon, GitHub or the content store does not answer THEN only its rows say "Unavailable" (or "Build copy") and the note names it.
- WHEN an ask fails THEN its banner shows until the next answer, and its details name the request id.
