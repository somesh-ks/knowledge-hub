## Working in production

- Engine end to end on live services: fetch (Exa, Firecrawl, feeds), judge (TypeSafe Jev), write (Nebius), validate, commit as the owner.
- Vercel deploys the site from the Git integration. CI on Linux: typecheck, lint, tests, site build, gitleaks (with `.gitleaks.toml` allowing the invented values tests assert on). Auto-merge for owner and Dependabot PRs.
- Reader in the visual language of Google Podcasts (D31): Today, Library, Create, Settings. Library filters are All, Daily, Weekly, Custom; Custom holds everything made on request.
- Player as one surface from the bottom bar to a full page (D32, D50), with seek, a clock and volume on wide screens. The whole bar opens it; a drag, the bar, Back or Escape close it (no chevron; a "Close the player" button shows only on keyboard focus). The open page: cover, title, story and source; the five transport controls on one axis with a speed pill under play on a phone, and the two skips with the speed between them on a wide screen with a mouse. The speed opens a slider from 0.5x to 2x in steps of 0.05, in a modal dialog (under the pill on a phone, above it with a mouse) with 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x and 2x presets; any step is remembered. Back closes the open player instead of leaving the page, and the desktop bar's line has a notch at the playhead. Timeline markers are one small dot per story. The scrubber's time bubble and the bar's times hold still while the slider moves: each sits over a hidden copy of the widest time it can show, and the bubble moves in whole device pixels. An episode with chapters gets a Chapters / Transcript switch; the transcript reads like Speechify (said words in full ink, the rest muted, the spoken word on a soft fill) from Speechify's word marks, with an estimate where a segment has none.
- Narration on Play, story by story (D33).
- Custom episodes (D37): the first real run of `podcast.yml` succeeded on 2026-10-08 and published `ep-20261008-1155`. Discussion episodes use a second voice by default (`dominic_32`). Twelve episodes a day, two minutes apart.
- Episode length: the writer is told the length in words and a script is held to what its stories can carry. Real dry runs on 2026-10-08 put 5 and 10 minute episodes at 83 to 90% of the length asked for (they were at 25 to 67%). `dispatch podcast --dry-run` measures one request without writing.
- Delete an episode, or regenerate it with feedback, from a three-dots menu on the episode page (D44). Both go through `/api/podcast` and `podcast.yml`. A delete takes effect at once on the device that asked: the episode is hidden there while the workflow and the redeploy finish.
- Nothing is said under the play chip before audio exists. Space plays and pauses from anywhere once the bar is there. The first Play on a page with no audio starts by itself (it used to spin until a second press).
- Moves between Today, Library, Create and Settings are answered from a copy the service worker saved in the last ten minutes and swapped at once (D47). Measured on production on 2026-10-09, medians on desktop and phone: the page arrives in 2 ms (was 26), the new page is on screen in 32 ms (was 70) and fully drawn in 132 ms (was 407). The current destination is marked the moment it is pressed.
- Model in Settings (D46): the writer's provider (Nebius or Anthropic) and model. With Nebius the list opens with six top writers by name (D58), then every live model, with a filter. The engine tries the chosen model first and falls back to the two variables.
- One top rhythm across the four destinations, held to 1px by `e2e/rhythm.spec.ts`; a sliding thumb on segmented controls that moves at once when a key moves it (a `data-input` mark on the root says whether the last input was a key or a pointer); every page can scroll its last control clear of the floating button. Every select (Settings, Library sort, Create) opens one drawn list set in `primitives.css` through `appearance: base-select` (Chromium 135+, Safari 27; native lists elsewhere), opening below its field when it fits.
- Ambient cover light (D48): a soft light in the cover's colours behind the header of Today and of an episode, drawn by a small WebGL canvas on a GPU and by a CSS layer everywhere else.
- Schedule in Settings (D45): hour, cadence and topics of the scheduled briefing. The Create audience "Customized" is called "Somesh mode".
- Create is the primary action: a side button on wide screens, a floating button on phones, a panel for the form (full screen on a phone, a side panel on desktop) and a compact progress indicator. The phone button shows only on Today and the Library (D52), extended (plus and "Create") at the top and while scrolling up and folded while scrolling down; pressing the tab of the page you are on scrolls it to the top. Enter in Create's Title puts the keyboard away instead of starting a run, and the Create panel closes only on a press that began on its scrim.
- General episodes built from 24 general-news feeds, ranked by coverage, with their own topics (D43).
- One Jev request per item and a per-run Firecrawl ceiling of 30 (D42).
- The site's GitHub token reaches the repository since 2026-10-08; before that Create, Generate and config saves all failed.
- The passphrase step is switched off (D41). Caps, cooldowns and the narration budget stay enforced.
- Five topics with Top picks always included, General and Customized audiences (D39); Design and Trending ship with the app, Trending ranked by coverage, discovery only suggests (D40); a 44-entry source catalogue feeds the add-topic flow.

- Making something shows live stages in the run indicator: seven for a briefing (one per workflow step, then "Updating the site"), three for an episode ("Getting ready", "Making the episode", "Updating the site"; `podcast.yml` makes the whole episode in one step, so nothing finer is shown). Toasts share one quiet style with an icon for success and failure.
- Interface sounds by kind of action (navigate, select, open and close, success, failure) through one delegated listener that loads the sound code on the first gesture and follows the Sounds setting.
- Today and episode pages carry one three-dots menu (Share, Regenerate, Delete on an episode); an episode page shows Highlights with links, not the script. The Library has a List view, search inside episode scripts, and a play button on covers. Settings is two levels (D51): an index (Daily, Weekly, Topics & Sources and Model rows with a one-line summary each; Interface sounds; Appearance; About) and one page per job, each titled with a breadcrumb ("Settings › Daily", 28px on a phone, 22px from 64rem); Topics & Sources lists its topics by name, Other last, one page each; a topic's page edits its details and adds the sources that suit it (D57). One panel (a full sheet on a phone, a side panel from 40rem) adds a topic, then offers the catalogue sources that suit it to tick and add at once, or adds a source by its address; a source's row opens the same panel to rename it, move it to another topic or open its address (D56). There is no Custom page: `/settings/custom` goes to `/create`. Changes save themselves (`scripts/autosave.ts`): 1.2 s after the last change, at most 8 ops a request, a removal waits 5 s with Undo, status in the app's toast, and the toast keeps the control just changed in sight. Old addresses redirect (`astro.config.mjs`). Form fields have a 3:1 outline and, in the player, only Play is in full ink (D54).
- Style tests forbid raw lengths for spacing, type size and radius, and reading a custom property nothing defines. The view transition overlay lets the pointer through, so the cursor does not flicker over a link.

- Writer providers (D49): Nebius and Anthropic (Claude Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5), chosen with a vendor select and a model combobox. A failed Anthropic pick falls through to the Nebius environment models.
- Schedule profiles: `config/schedule.json` holds the Daily fields, a `weekly` profile (day, hour, type, length, voices, on/off) and a `custom` profile (what the Create panel starts from; no Settings page since D56, so it changes only in the file). The engine's weekly run follows its own day, hour and length.
- Sources: a score out of 3 from health, quality and trust, combined as a geometric mean (D55; New under 10 judged items, trust "not measured yet" under 10 checked items), drawn as three pips beside the number on each row, with its parts in the edit panel. `GET /api/source-preview` (name, icon, feed, latest post, score and its parts) is behind an SSRF guard. A site with no icon shows the first letter of its name. A move or rename from the panel is `source.update`; a move also sets the source's category hint in a run.
- Voices: `GET /api/voices`; custom episodes are narrated in the voices of their request, briefings in the schedule's (a schedule change reaches Play after a redeploy).
- The writer is told the hosts are Ada and Leo. Settings: no default-speed row (the player keeps the last speed), "Interface sounds", dividers inside the content. ## Known risks

- With the passphrase off, anyone who can open the site can start a briefing, an episode, narration or a config edit, inside the caps (D41).
- Budget counters can read below real spend in narrow cases: an edit that does not lengthen a briefing, two briefings charged in the same instant, a function killed mid-synthesis. The engine workflow and the site function keep separate counters against one Speechify allowance.
- `markSeen` records every judged id, so items cut by the cap are hidden for 90 days.
- `podcast.yml` and `config.yml` push to main beside `run.yml`; the push rebases and retries, which has not been exercised.
- Every session builds into `apps/web/dist`; two sessions running browser suites at once give false failures.
