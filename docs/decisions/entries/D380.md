## D380: the Dispatch rename is closed out: the skill and the old-state moves are gone

- Status: supersedes D16; partly supersedes D179
- Date: 2026-11-06 (the owner, in the rename thread: "remove skill", "the skill is deleted already", and "remove" for the old-state moves)
- Decision: the personal skill `sk-dispatch` (D16) no longer exists; the owner deleted it, the runbook no longer sends anyone to it, and the in-repo skills `digest` and `learn` are the only ones. D179's one-time moves are removed: the head script `storageRename.js` (and its Content-Security-Policy hash) that renamed `dispatch:*` keys, and the copy of downloads from `dispatch-audio-v1` in the page and the service worker. The worker's activation still deletes any cache named `dispatch-*`, so an old copy does not linger. Everything else in D179 stands, and `workflow_dispatch` and the verb stay.
- Why: 13 days passed since #272 and the owner is the only user; the moves served a device that had not opened the site since. A device that has not, loses its theme, played marks and story votes and its saved downloads; nothing else.
- Rejected: renaming the skill to `sk-singularity` (he did not want to keep it); keeping the moves longer (they only matter to a device he has not opened in weeks).
- Revisit if: a device turns up with the old keys or downloads. Old `dispatch:*` keys stay where they are, so their values can be copied to `singularity:*` by hand without overwriting newer ones; the old downloads cache is deleted on activation and cannot be recovered.
