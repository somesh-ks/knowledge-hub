# Dock swipes: quiet, and a visible target (2026-11-03)

D359. Somesh: swiping the player dock up and down played the open and close sounds, and a sideways swipe gave no hint of the podcast it would switch to.

Sounds. The surface controller now says how a commit was decided (`drag` or `command`); a drag is felt through its haptic and not heard. A sheet pulled down (`pullDismiss.ts`) or swiped away (Base UI's `swipe` reason, `SheetFrame.tsx`) calls `quietNextClose()` (`sound/gestureQuiet.ts`), and the close cue that follows takes the note once. Follow-up: dragging the seek line no longer ticks; each item boundary is a detent haptic only. A tap, a key and a button keep their cues. A browser test counts oscillators across a tap, an up swipe and a down swipe, and failed with the old code.

Peek. `useDockSwipe` draws the neighbour (cover, title, kind) as `.dock-peek`, one bar's width off the side and moving with the finger; the bar clips to its own box. Past the swipe rule both slide on over 250 ms, the podcast is asked for at once, and the slide is dropped when the player shows it, never before the slide has landed. Once the finger is sideways the surface controller's gesture ends, so a wander up never pulls the page.

Gotchas: a grid item placed on cells the thumb and words used to auto-place pushed them to a second row, so they are placed now; the peek uses `contain: size` so a long title cannot widen the wide bar.

Fade (2026-11-05). Somesh: "the current track item slowly fade out and the next one fade in, in both directions". `follow` sets `--dock-fade` (0 to 1, the finger's travel over the bar's width) next to `--dock-swipe`; the bar's cover and words take `1 - fade`, the peek takes `fade`. The slide stays, so the fade rides the finger and finishes with the slide on `--duration-enter` (250 ms, the tabs' slide); let go short, both fade back on the press curve. Reduced motion zeroes the travel and keeps the fade: both stay put and cross-fade in place. If he wants it slower, it is the one `--duration-enter` pair in `player.css` and `DOCK_SLIDE_MS`.

Title and progress (2026-11-06). Somesh: the bar's title had become the chapter; it should be the podcast's name, with the story's short name after the clock in place of the section, and the progress line should replace the hairline above the navigation. `PlayerBar.tsx` now titles the bar `digest.title`, the caption shows the story's chapter (headline if none) and the button's name holds all three. Below 64rem the `.dock-progress` line sits on the bar's bottom edge on a hairline-coloured track and the controls centre above it; from 64rem it stays on top, where the seek slider uses it.

Ring (2026-11-06). Somesh saw an outline around the bar on his phone. The global `:focus-visible` ring drew on it; the bar takes focus from script when the page closes, and a ring a key gave it stays while it has focus. `player.css` hides the bar's ring unless `html[data-input]` (app.ts) reads `keyboard`, so a keyboard keeps it. Not reproduced from a touch close in Chromium; the guard is a key-given ring going when a finger touches. Other controls keep the global rule; the same switch could move into `foundations.css`.
