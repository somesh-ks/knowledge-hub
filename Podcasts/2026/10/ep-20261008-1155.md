# Singularity: Top picks, Interaction craft, AI models and products, Launches

A: This is Singularity.

B: Today's top story: the Modern design system uses DESIGN.md and tokens.css.

A: It separates design intent in DESIGN.md from implementation in tokens.css. Semantic tokens compile to Tailwind v4 and validate component manifests.

B: So DESIGN.md holds the intent, and tokens.css handles the code?

A: Yes. Color, typography, and spacing follow predefined scales. Hover states use OKLab.

B: That could improve consistency across teams.

A: Next, @shadcn/registry@0.1.3 now loads ts-morph only for writes.

B: Does that reduce bundle size?

A: Yes. Read-only bundles are 6 MB smaller. Patch updates support transformers for fonts, RTL, and CSS variables without ts-morph.

B: That makes the tool faster for common use cases.

A: Amazon Style Dictionary now integrates with Tailwind CSS.

B: What does that enable?

A: Design tokens map to Tailwind utilities. It ensures consistent color, spacing, and typography across platforms.

B: That simplifies cross-platform design.

A: 78.1% of DESIGN.md files lack YAML tokens.

B: What does that mean for design systems?

A: Of 1,162 public files, 908 have no YAML front matter. Design values stay in unstructured markdown. 747 still list three or more hex colors inline.

B: That makes automation harder.

A: llm-mistral 0.16 adds Mistral Large 4 support.

B: What's new in this version?

A: It supports Mistral's reasoning models through the Mistral API. Adds access to Mistral Large 4 for structured reasoning tasks.

B: That expands its use in complex workflows.

A: Caddis 1.1.0 ships with particles and graph loops.

B: What do particles do?

A: They use Point Solver with state retention. Support gravity, drag, collision, and flocking behaviors.

B: And what about graph loops?

A: Loops enable feedback, For Each operations, and frame-to-frame persistence.

B: That adds dynamic behavior to visual scripts.

A: That's all for this episode.
