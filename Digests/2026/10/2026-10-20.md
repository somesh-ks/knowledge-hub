### [Walking 3,000 steps a day was linked to slower Alzheimer's tau buildup](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261018205058.htm)
ScienceDaily Science

A study of almost 300 older adults with early Alzheimer's brain signs found walking slowed tau, a misfolded protein tied to decline. Benefits began near 3,000 steps a day, about half an hour of moderate walking, and levelled off past 7,500. Dementia signs fell almost by half in more active people, though walking did not reduce amyloid plaques. The group was small, mostly white and well educated, and other lifestyle factors were not accounted for.
