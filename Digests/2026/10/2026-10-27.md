### [A guide pairs Style Dictionary tokens with Tailwind to keep code consistent](https://alfa.razon.com.mx/building-organic-design-consistency-at-scale-amazon-style-dictionary-tailwind-css)
alfa.razon.com.mx, Clara Fischer

A guide pairs Amazon's Style Dictionary, a central store of design tokens, with Tailwind CSS to keep design and code aligned. Colors, spacing and type are defined once, then mapped to Tailwind utilities, with spacing on a scale of 0.25rem, 0.5rem and 1rem. The same tokens can be translated for React, Android and iOS code, and can drive light, dark and high-contrast themes. The guide says organizations report faster time to market but gives no figures.

### [OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Terra puts a midrange price on vision and computer use](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/26/gpt-61-terra)
Simon Willison

OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Terra, a mid-priced model that leads its announcement with visual reasoning and computer use. It costs $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output, almost half the price of GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol, and about ten times GPT-6 Luna. OpenAI's Ted Sanders says it is similar to Astra and Sol on vision and computer use, but much faster. He also says it is a bit worse on most other things.

### [Korea fines Samsung 20 billion won over throttled Galaxy gaming performance](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026102711431135088)
asiae.co.kr, Oh Yukyo

Korea's Fair Trade Commission fined Samsung about 20 billion won for advertising Galaxy gaming speed while throttling it. A throttling policy called GOS cut CPU and GPU speeds to as low as 30% of maximum in certain games on the Galaxy S21, S22 and Tab S8. Related sales were about 4 trillion won, and the penalty was expected near 60 billion won until Samsung's software update lifting the limits lowered the severity.
