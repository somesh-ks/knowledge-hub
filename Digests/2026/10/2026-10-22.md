## Science

### [Orangutans prefer food linked to the sound of laughter](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/oct/21/orangutans-prefer-food-laughter-sounds-study)
The Guardian Science, Nicola Davis Science correspondent

Orangutans valued food more when it was tied to the sound of laughing peers, a study in Biology Letters found. Six orangutans at two zoos learned to find treats under materials such as moss or sawdust, each paired with either recorded laughter or wind. When offered both, they overall picked the laughter-linked one, which the senior author says could reflect a better mood while learning. Only four of the six showed a clear preference.
