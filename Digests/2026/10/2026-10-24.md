### [CSS finally gets native masonry layouts through display: grid-lanes](https://piccalil.li/blog/native-masonry-layouts-with-css-grid-lanes)
Piccalilli, Saleh Mubashar

The CSS Working Group settled on display: grid-lanes as the name for native masonry layouts, a design that packs items into columns of uneven height. The group agreed in January 2025 to reuse Grid's track-definition and placement properties, and had the name by December 2025. Developers define columns and the browser places each item in a suitable lane, usually the shortest, with no item heights needed. The specification is still an Editor's Draft, so details can change.

## Science

### [A two-year trial finds fish oil did nothing for older adults' memory](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/10/261020085427.htm)
ScienceDaily Science

A two-year trial by Keck Medicine of USC found that high-dose fish oil did not improve memory or thinking, or prevent shrinkage of the hippocampus, a memory region. It enrolled 365 adults aged 55 to 80 at higher risk of Alzheimer's, who took 2,000 mg of DHA daily or a placebo. DHA in spinal fluid rose 17% after six months, so it reached the brain. The results apply to this group only.
